1. Remove the germ (the center part) from the [garlic](i:garlic) cloves. Use 1-2 cloves, or more to taste.
2. Put the [garlic](i:garlic), the [egg](i:egg) and a splash of [olive oil](i:olive_oil) in a tall glass, ideal for the [immersion blender](tool:immersion_blender).
3. Blend everything with the [immersion blender](tool:immersion_blender).
4. Without stopping, start adding [olive oil](i:olive_oil) slowly in a thin stream, the whole time, until it emulsifies into a thick allioli.
