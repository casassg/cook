1. Spoon the chilled [gazpacho](i:tomato_soup) into a bowl and scatter in the [croutons](i:croutons).
2. Quarter or chop the [eggs](i:eggs) and arrange them on top.
3. Finish with a drizzle of [olive oil](i:olive_oil) and a grind of pepper if you like.
