1. Mince the [garlic](i:garlic) finely. {variant: classic}
2. In a [medium bowl](tool:bowl), whisk the [garlic](i:garlic), [anchovy paste](i:anchovy_paste), [lemon juice](i:lemon), [Dijon mustard](i:dijon_mustard) and [Worcestershire sauce](i:worcestershire_sauce) with a [whisk](tool:whisk). {variant: classic}
3. Whisk in the [mayonnaise](i:mayonnaise), [Parmigiano-Reggiano](i:parmesan), [salt](i:salt) and [black pepper](i:black_pepper) until smooth and well combined. {variant: classic}
4. Taste and adjust — add more [lemon juice](i:lemon), [salt](i:salt) or [black pepper](i:black_pepper) to your liking. {variant: classic}
5. Serve right away, or cover and refrigerate until ready to use. It keeps for about a week and thickens as it chills; whisk in a teaspoon or two of [water](i:water) to thin it back out if needed. {variant: classic}
6. Add the [Greek yogurt](i:greek_yogurt), [tahini](i:tahini), [lemon juice](i:lemon), [Parmigiano-Reggiano](i:parmesan), [garlic](i:garlic), [Dijon mustard](i:dijon_mustard), [anchovy paste](i:anchovy_paste), [salt](i:salt) and [black pepper](i:black_pepper) to a [medium bowl](tool:bowl) and whisk until smooth and creamy. {variant: healthy}
7. Thin with [water](i:water), one tablespoon at a time, until it reaches the consistency you like. {variant: healthy}
8. Taste and adjust — add more [lemon juice](i:lemon), [salt](i:salt) or [black pepper](i:black_pepper) to your liking. {variant: healthy}
9. Serve right away, or cover and refrigerate; it keeps for up to 2 weeks. {variant: healthy}
