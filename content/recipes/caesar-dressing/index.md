1. Mince the [garlic](i:garlic) finely.
2. In a [medium bowl](tool:bowl), whisk the [garlic](i:garlic), [anchovy paste](i:anchovy_paste), [lemon juice](i:lemon), [Dijon mustard](i:dijon_mustard) and [Worcestershire sauce](i:worcestershire_sauce) with a [whisk](tool:whisk).
3. Whisk in the [mayonnaise](i:mayonnaise), [Parmigiano-Reggiano](i:parmesan), [salt](i:salt) and [black pepper](i:black_pepper) until smooth and well combined.
4. Taste and adjust — add more [lemon juice](i:lemon), [salt](i:salt) or [black pepper](i:black_pepper) to your liking.
5. Serve right away, or cover and refrigerate until ready to use. It keeps for about a week and thickens as it chills; whisk in a teaspoon or two of water to thin it back out if needed.
