1. Preheat the [oven](tool:oven) to 350°F (175°C).
2. Beat the [butter](i:butter), [white sugar](i:white_sugar) and [brown sugar](i:brown_sugar) with an [electric mixer](tool:mixer) in a [large bowl](tool:bowl) until smooth.
3. Beat in the [eggs](i:eggs) one at a time, then stir in the [vanilla extract](i:vanilla).
4. Dissolve the [baking soda](i:baking_soda) in the [hot water](i:water) and add it to the batter along with the [salt](i:salt).
5. Stir in the [flour](i:flour), [chocolate chips](i:chocolate_chips) and [walnuts](i:walnuts).
6. Drop spoonfuls of dough 2 inches apart onto ungreased [baking sheets](tool:baking_sheet).
7. Bake for [10 minutes](t:10m), until the edges are nicely browned.
8. Cool on the [baking sheets](tool:baking_sheet) briefly, then move to a [wire rack](tool:wire_rack) to cool completely.
