1. Toast the [noodles](i:noodles) in the [pan](tool:pan) with a good splash of [olive oil](i:olive_oil) over medium heat until golden, with a pinch of [salt](i:salt).
2. Heat the [fish stock](i:stock).
3. Add the [squid in American sauce](i:calamari) and the [clams](i:clams), stir, and pour in the hot [stock](i:stock).
4. Cook the [noodles](i:noodles) for [8 min](t:8m).
5. Arrange the [stuffed squid](i:stuffed_calamari) on top, cover the pan and let it rest for [2 min](t:2m). Finish with a good spoonful of [allioli](/recipes/allioli) on top.
