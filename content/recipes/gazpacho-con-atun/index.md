1. Spoon the chilled [gazpacho](i:tomato_soup) into a large bowl.
2. Scatter the [croutons](i:croutons) on top and let them soak slightly while you open the [tuna](i:tuna).
3. Flake the [tuna](i:tuna) over the soup and finish with a drizzle of [olive oil](i:olive_oil) if you like.
