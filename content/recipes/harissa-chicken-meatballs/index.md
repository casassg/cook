1. Preheat the [oven](tool:oven) to 400°F (200°C).
2. In a [large mixing bowl](tool:bowl), mix the [ground chicken](i:chicken), [chopped onion](i:onion), [panko](i:panko), [ricotta](i:ricotta), [egg](i:egg), [harissa](i:harissa), [garlic](i:garlic), [paprika](i:paprika), [cumin](i:cumin) and [salt](i:salt) until well combined.
3. Scoop 1 tbsp portions onto a [baking sheet](tool:baking_sheet), about 40 small meatballs.
4. Bake for [10-15 min](t:10m), until the internal temperature reaches 165°F (74°C).
5. Serve over rice or in a salad bowl.
