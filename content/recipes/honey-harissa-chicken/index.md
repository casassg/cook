1. Preheat the [air fryer](tool:air_fryer) to 375°F (190°C).
2. In a [small bowl](tool:bowl), mix the [harissa](i:harissa), [honey](i:honey), [olive oil](i:olive_oil), [salt](i:salt) and [black pepper](i:black_pepper).
3. Rub the mixture over the [chicken thighs](i:chicken_thighs) on all sides.
4. Place the [chickpeas](i:chickpeas) in the air fryer basket and top with the chicken, skin side up.
5. Cook for [18-20 min](t:18m), until the chicken is cooked through and the chickpeas are crispy.
6. Squeeze the [lemon](i:lemon) over the chicken and chickpeas just before serving.
