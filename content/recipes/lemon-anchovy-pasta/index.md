1. Boil the [pasta](i:pasta) in a [large pot](tool:large_pot) with **less water than usual** to concentrate the starch — this is what makes the final emulsion glossy. Undercook by about 1 minute, it finishes in the pan.

2. Slice the [garlic](i:garlic) and chop the [anchovies](i:anchovies) while the pasta cooks.

3. Reserve a cup of the starchy pasta water, then drain.

4. In a [pan](tool:pan) off the heat, toss the pasta with the [pecorino](i:pecorino), the [garlic](i:garlic), the [anchovies](i:anchovies) and a splash of the pasta water. Toss hard: the starch emulsifies with the cheese and the anchovies dissolve, leaving deep umami and no fishiness. Loosen with more pasta water, a splash at a time, if it tightens.

5. Off the heat, add the [lemon](i:lemon) zest and juice and toss once more. Heat dulls the citrus, so the lemon only ever goes in at the very end.

6. Finish with chopped [parsley](i:parsley) and lots of [black pepper](i:black_pepper), taste and adjust, and serve immediately.
