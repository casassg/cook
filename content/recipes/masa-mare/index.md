1. Day 1: in a [glass jar](tool:jar), mix ½ cup [bread flour](i:flour) with ½ cup [warm water](i:water) into a thick paste.
2. Cover the [jar](tool:jar) loosely — not airtight — and leave it at room temperature for [24 hours](t:24h).
3. Day 2: stir in another ½ cup [bread flour](i:flour) and ½ cup [warm water](i:water).
4. Cover loosely and rest for [24 hours](t:24h).
5. Day 3: you may see bubbles. Discard half of the mixture.
6. Feed with ½ cup [bread flour](i:flour) and ½ cup [warm water](i:water), mix well, cover loosely and rest for [24 hours](t:24h).
7. Days 4–7: discard half and feed with ½ cup [bread flour](i:flour) plus ½ cup [warm water](i:water) every 12–24 hours.
8. It is ready when it reliably doubles 4–8 hours after feeding: drop a spoonful in water — if it floats, bake with it.
9. Maintenance: once active, scoop out two-thirds to bake with, leaving the rest in the [jar](tool:jar).
10. Add 3–4 tablespoons of [bread flour](i:flour).
11. Stir in [warm water](i:water) with a [spoon](tool:spoon) until it looks like thin liquid yogurt, about half a [jar](tool:jar).
12. Leave it at room temperature for [5–6 hours](t:5h), not fully covered, until it rises.
13. Once it has risen and fallen back down, put the [jar](tool:jar) in the fridge; it keeps for up to 6 days. Feed it again before baking.
