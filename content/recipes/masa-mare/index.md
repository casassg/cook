1. Scoop two-thirds of the [starter](i:starter) out of the [jar](tool:jar) to bake with, leaving the rest behind.
2. Add 3–4 tablespoons of [bread flour](i:flour).
3. Stir in [warm water](i:warm_water) with a [spoon](tool:spoon) until the mixture looks like thin liquid yogurt, about half a [jar](tool:jar).
4. Leave it at room temperature for [5–6 hours](t:5h), not fully covered, until it rises.
5. Once it has risen and fallen back down, put the [jar](tool:jar) in the fridge; it keeps for up to 6 days.
