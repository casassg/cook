1. The evening before: in a [large bowl](tool:bowl), mix the [bread flour](i:bread_flour), [water](i:water) and [olive oil](i:olive_oil) with a [wooden spoon](tool:wooden_spoon) until homogeneous, then keep mixing for at least 2 more minutes — finish with your hands at the end, once the dough gets less sticky.
2. Rest for [10 minutes](t:10m).
3. Add the [salt](i:salt) and [dry active yeast](i:yeast) and knead with your hands for 3–5 minutes, until the dough turns elastic.
4. Cover the [bowl](tool:bowl) with [plastic wrap](tool:plastic_wrap) and rest for [1 hour](t:1h).
5. Uncover, knead for 1–2 minutes inside the [bowl](tool:bowl), cover again and rest for another [hour](t:1h).
6. Uncover, knead for 2 minutes and cover again.
7. Let the dough ferment until it has tripled in volume — 3 to 8 hours; overnight in a cool, draft-free spot is the easy route.
8. The next morning: uncover the dough; it should have tripled. Turn it out onto a floured work surface and flatten it lightly with your hands.
9. Fold two opposite sides inward, one over the other like a letter, then repeat with the remaining two sides.
10. Flip the dough over and tuck the edges underneath while turning it slowly, until you have a smooth round ball.
11. Line a clean [bowl](tool:bowl) with a floured [kitchen cloth](tool:cloth), place the ball smooth-side up inside, dust with flour and wrap the cloth over it like a parcel.
12. Rest for [1 hour](t:1h).
13. Meanwhile, preheat the [oven](tool:oven) to 450°F with the [baking tray](tool:baking_tray) inside, and place an [oven-safe dish](tool:steam_dish) with water on the lower rack to create steam.
14. Take the hot [tray](tool:baking_tray) out, dust it with [flour](i:plain_flour), flip the dough onto it smooth-side down and score a deep cross on top.
15. Bake for [20 minutes](t:20m), then lower the oven to 400°F and remove the water dish.
16. Bake for at least [30 more minutes](t:30m) until golden — up to 20 extra minutes for a darker crust.
17. Move the loaves to a [wire rack](tool:wire_rack) and cool for at least [10 minutes](t:10m), ideally 40, before slicing.
