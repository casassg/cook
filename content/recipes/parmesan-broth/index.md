1. Put the [vegetable broth](i:vegetable_broth), [Parmesan rinds](i:parmesan_rinds), [mushroom stalks](i:mushroom_stems), [onion](i:onion), [garlic](i:garlic), [bay leaf](i:bay_leaves) and a pinch of [black peppercorns](i:black_pepper) in a [large pot](tool:pot).

2. Bring to a simmer over medium heat, then lower the heat and cover. Simmer gently for [40 min](t:40m) until the broth tastes deeply savoury.

3. Strain through a [fine-mesh sieve](tool:strainer) and discard the solids. Keep the broth warm for the risotto, or let it cool and freeze for later.
