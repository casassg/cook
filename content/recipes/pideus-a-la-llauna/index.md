1. Slice the [garlic](i:garlic) thin; this is the only real cooking.
2. Add a drizzle of [olive oil](i:olive_oil) to the [pan](tool:pan) and sauté the [garlic](i:garlic) and the [noodles](i:noodles) with a pinch of [salt](i:salt), until they start to change color.
3. Take them off the heat.
4. The hidden trick: the [stock](i:stock). Add 700 ml of good [stock](i:stock) and bring it to a boil: this is where all the flavor is.
5. Add the canned [stuffed calamari](i:calamari) in American sauce with all its sauce: here's the essence, the juice.
6. Add the [clams](i:clams) with their juice and integrate everything into the hot [stock](i:stock).
7. Add the [baby squid](i:xipirons), cover, and let it finish.
8. Serve with the [allioli](/recipes/allioli) on top.
