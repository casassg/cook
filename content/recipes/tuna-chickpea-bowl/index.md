1. Drain and rinse the [chickpeas](i:chickpeas) and put them in a bowl.
2. Add the drained [tuna](i:tuna) and flake it with a fork.
3. Add the chopped [tomato](i:tomato) and the [croutons](i:croutons).
4. Dress with the [olive oil](i:olive_oil) and a dusting of [smoked paprika](i:smoked_paprika), and toss.
